# Background

## Who is the player?

Engineers sent from another planet to construct a superweapon in secret.

## Why are they on the planet?

They fled there to build a space ship capable of fighting their enemies, sentient nanomachines.

## How did they get there?

They were teleported there, using different technology from the stargates. This tech reconstructs only the organic matter on the other end, but does not physically transfer matter.

## Why are they building/automating/mining things?

To build the ship, which must be highly advanced to be a threat to the nanomachines and must also be built in secret and isolation. Since it must be so far away to avoid detection, it must be advanced enough to both combat the nanomachines and also travel enormous distances in a short time.

## Why do they have no technology (or the tech they carried with them)?

Any technology or non-organic matter could be compromised, and can not be brought using the teleportation technique used.

## Why are there not more of them?

The technique is very expensive and can only be used a limited amount. Might destroy the device or even planet to do it, so could only be used once.
