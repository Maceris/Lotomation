# Processed Materials

- Steel - Coal + Iron
- Bronze - 3 Copper + 1 Tin = 4 bronze (even though 7:1 is more accurate, it’s an annoying ratio that nerfs Tin too much)
- Plastic - Oil processing
- Glass - Quartz processing
- Concrete - Limestone processing
- Sticky Resin - Macerating wood
- Rubber - Extractor on sticky resin gives 2, smelting gives 1
