# Side Branches

The branches beside the two main tracks ([Tech Branch](Tech%20Branch.md) and [Magic Branch](Magic%20Branch.md)). The overview and the maps are in [Tiers](Tiers.md#side-branches); this doc has each branch in detail. Nothing here is decided.

**Exposed early, required later.** A branch should feel optional when it appears: it makes things easier, cheaper, or prettier, and the player can ignore it for a while. But something **late** in each branch becomes part of the **new mechanic** a later tier introduces, so finishing the game needs every branch, the way Factorio needs every science pack. The new mechanic is *built from* the branch, so the branch is part of the idea, not a toll on top of it. Most branches are exposed by a tier on each track, so a player who leans on magic still meets every branch.

Casino and Combat are the exceptions: they stay optional for the whole game.

# Power

- **Exposed from**: Clockwork (T3), Galvanic (M4)
- **Required by**: Sunforge (T11). Startup power for a fusion reactor, and storage to buffer it. Magic's Steam and Lightning generators can supply it.
- **Covers**: Every power source: combustion, steam, solar, wind, geothermal, hydro, fission, fusion. Storage: batteries, flywheels, antimatter.
- **Feeds the tracks with**: Generators and storage for every [power grade](Tech%20Branch.md#power-grades).
- **Magic side**: Steam and Lightning turn essence into power, and Magitech (M4) converts both ways, so a magic wing can power a tech wing.
- **A choice of path**: every source works, but each has a different footprint, maintenance profile and byproduct. A geothermal-heavy base looks nothing like a solar-heavy one.

# Logistics

- **Exposed from**: Clockwork (T3, M3)
- **Required by**: Zenith (T10, M10). Launch logistics on both tracks: rates to an orbital shipyard, and essence flow for sky rituals (Motion).
- **Covers**: Belt and pipe tiers, splitters and filters ([Transport](../Types%20of%20Things/Transport.md)), trains, drones, late wireless with range and bandwidth limits.
- **Feeds the tracks with**: Belts, pipes, trains and drones.
- **Magic side**: Essences are fluids in the same pipes; Motion and Prana speed up flow; familiars (Summoning, M11) do small, awkward jobs.
- **Never trivialized**: late logistics makes logistics bigger and more interesting, never one block that does everything (the AE2 and Ender IO lesson).
- Familiars stay a *different* option, not a better one: limited capacity and range, upkeep, slow.

# Metallurgy

- **Exposed from**: Crucible (T2, M2)
- **Required by**: Athanor (T6, M6). Coil alloys for heat as a stat, and Forge-tempered metal for infusion strength.
- **Covers**: Alloys, coils, heat treatment.
- **Feeds the tracks with**: Better machine, coil and cable materials.
- **Magic side**: Metal and Forge essences; infused metals for tools and coils.
- **Each material should matter**: a metal earns its place with a distinct job (heat resistance, conductivity, purity), not a stat bump.

# Chemistry

- **Exposed from**: Alembic (T5, M5)
- **Required by**: Nexus (T8). Solvents and fluorine chemistry for isotope separation.
- **Covers**: Distillation, electrolysis, catalysts, polymers, acids.
- **Feeds the tracks with**: Plastics, solvents, rare fractions from separation.
- **Magic side**: Alchemy is the same skill on the magic side: bulk reactions, potions, and Null as a byproduct (neutralized by Balance, refined by Rot). Sulfur later works as a catalyst.
- The first real **byproduct problem**: distillation gives several products at once, and each needs a use or a sink.

# Electronics

- **Exposed from**: Galvanic (T4, M4)
- **Required by**: Oracle (T9, M9). Processors for the advanced assembler, and Arcanum boards for Spell Engines.
- **Covers**: Circuits through each generation: vacuum tube, transistor, integrated, processor, quantum, biological.
- **Feeds the tracks with**: The circuits every machine needs.
- **Magic side**: Arcanum conductors and Synthesis circuitry.
- **Biological circuits** could be an alternate to silicon at some point, not a replacement.

# Biology

- **Exposed from**: Crucible (T2), Alembic (M5)
- **Required by**: Exodus (C3). Crew systems: life support, food, grown organic parts (Vitality).
- **Covers**: Farms, tree farms, fungi, bacteria cultures, **bioleaching** (microbes pull metals from crushed rock), genetics. Tentative in [Ideas](Ideas.md).
- **Feeds the tracks with**: A renewable alternate path for some metals and chemicals, and the organic goods the [Mechanics](Mechanics.md) lore uses.
- **Magic side**: Wood, Life, Rot and Vitality essences, and [magical crops](Magic%20Branch.md#magical-crops), where Biology and Magic meet.
- **Metals by separation or by biology**: copper from a crusher-sifter-washer chain, or from bioleaching cultures. Both are fine.

# Computation

- **Exposed from**: Oracle (T9, M9)
- **Required by**: Magnum Opus (C2). Matter patterns: transmutation needs the data recipe of every element it makes.
- **Covers**: Research data, logic programs, factory control.
- **Feeds the tracks with**: Data recipes for the advanced assembler.
- **Magic side**: Lore and Gnosis divination as data, and Spell Engines as programmable magic.
- Required for **research data**, never for programming. Programmable logic stays an optional depth layer (the ComputerCraft complaint in [Modded Minecraft](../Prior%20Art/Modded%20Minecraft.md)).

# Construction

- **Exposed from**: Arrival
- **Required by**: Prism (T7, M7). Cleanrooms and warded rooms are both buildings: sealed walls, airlocks, ward stones.
- **Covers**: Decorative blocks, building tools, structural materials, lighting.
- **Feeds the tracks with**: Cleanroom walls, shielding, shipyard structures.
- **Magic side**: Decoration raises magic's aura ([Beauty and Harmony](Magic%20Branch.md#beauty-and-harmony)), and wards are placed blocks.
- Keeps the "freedom to build and decorate" pillar a real branch, with a functional reward.

# Exploration

- **Exposed from**: Arrival
- **Required by**: Paradox (C1). Derelict nanomachine fragments, reverse-engineered for the shield generator.
- **Covers**: Surveying, maps, derelicts, lore logs, relics ([Mechanics](Mechanics.md)).
- **Feeds the tracks with**: Alternate recipes found in the world (like Satisfactory hard drives), shortcuts.
- **Magic side**: Ley lines and relics feed magic; high-aura places are found by exploring.

# Casino

- **Exposed from**: Arrival
- **Required by**: never. Optional.
- **Covers**: The whole [Mechanics](Mechanics.md) gacha layer: slots, pachinko, auto-pullers, gems. Fake currency only.
- **Feeds the tracks with**: Small bonuses from gems and relics, cosmetics, and parody.
- **Magic side**: Chaos and Distortion, for luck.
- **Never required**: it parodies predatory design, so it must never gate progress.

# Combat

- **Exposed from**: Arrival
- **Required by**: never. Optional.
- **Covers**: Weapons, armor, turrets, the Nanomachine Incursions from [Mechanics](Mechanics.md).
- **Feeds the tracks with**: Nothing required. Combat only gates combat things.
- **Magic side**: Wrath essence, which also has peaceful uses: demolition, shredding, cutting tools.
- The ship's weapon capstone is built and installed, never fired by the player, so it needs no combat.

# Specialization (mastery)

The **Mastery Board** in [Mechanics](Mechanics.md) (Albion's Destiny Board) fits here. Each branch has mastery levels earned from the **factory's throughput** in that branch, not from clicks. Mastery gives small bonuses inside the branch (faster machines, less waste), plus titles and cosmetics.

In co-op, branches give players a natural way to divide work: one person runs power, another runs chemistry. In solo play, the player does them all at their own pace.
