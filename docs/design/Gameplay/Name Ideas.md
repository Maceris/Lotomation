# Name Ideas

Naming brainstorms for the progression: era and tier names, names for groups of eras, and power grade names. Nothing here is picked unless it says so. The tiers themselves are in [Tiers](Tiers.md), [Tech Branch](Tech%20Branch.md) and [Magic Branch](Magic%20Branch.md).

---

# Era Names

The tech tier names are placeholders from real-world history. Some alternatives, organized by theme. A name should say what the era *introduces*, and ideally fit both tracks, since each row has a tech tier and a magic tier. The current pick for the shared row names is the blend column ([Era Redesign](Tiers.md#the-progression)).

**Workplace** names each era after its iconic machine or place: "the Dynamo era", "reaching the Cleanroom". Concrete and easy to remember, and the quest tab icon picks itself. Neutral between tech and magic.

**Ship stage** names each era after the capstone, so the era name and the progress bar are the same thing. Strong on story, but it tells the player less about the new mechanic.

**Tech and magic blend** names fit both tracks at once, often by borrowing a word from alchemy or early science that sounds like both:

- **Hearth**: the first fire, folk charms, home.
- **Crucible**: where metals are alloyed and essences first pulled out.
- **Clockwork**: belts, and glyphs that run on their own.
- **Galvanic**: Galvani's twitching frog legs, electricity that looked like magic. Fits Magitech and Lightning.
- **Alembic**: the alchemist's still. Distillation is the Industrial Age's core process, and Balance and Rot deal with Null, magic's byproduct.
- **Athanor**: the alchemist's furnace, built to hold a steady heat for weeks. Heat and infusion strength as stats.
- **Prism**: lenses, wafers, Crystal, purity, clean and warded rooms.
- **Nexus**, **Isotope** or **Decay**: the reactor core and the ley line nexus, both placement puzzles around a dangerous center; Oblivion as decay pushed to its extreme.
- **Oracle**: computers that know things, and divination as data (Lore, Gnosis).
- **Zenith**: the top of the sky, the first launch.
- **Sunforge**: a star held in a reactor, a great spirit held in a circle; Ruin's plasma.
- **Paradox**: matter that shouldn't exist, held in fields, and Eldritch essences at full strength.
- **Magnum Opus**: alchemy's Great Work, completed with the Philosopher's Stone, matching matter from energy.
- **Exodus**: leaving.

Other single-theme sets to consider:

- **Materials**: Flint, Bronze, Iron, Copper, Polymer, Titanium, Silicon, Uranium, Lanthanide, Carbon, Helium, Antimatter, Quintessence. Clear, but they collide with item names in recipes.
- **Celestial**: Ember, Candle, Lantern, Beacon, Dawn, Sun, Nova... already proposed for magic grades in [Power Grades](Tech%20Branch.md#power-grades), so better kept there.
- **Myth and legend**: Prometheus (fire), Hephaestus (forge), Daedalus (machines), Icarus (flight)... evocative, but mostly Greek and a bit grand.

## Names for groups of eras

The four ranges in [How Big Is "Big"](Tiers.md#how-big-is-big) could get names too, shown as quest book sections above the tier tabs. A range covers the same tiers on both tracks:

| Range     | Eras              | Alchemy's Great Work                        | Wu Xing seasons   | Times of day |
| --------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------ |
| Early     | Arrival to tier 3 | Nigredo (blackening: breaking down)         | Spring (Wood)     | Dawn         |
| Mid       | Tiers 4 to 7      | Albedo (whitening: purifying)               | Summer (Fire)     | Day          |
| Late      | Tiers 8 to 11     | Citrinitas (yellowing: the solar dawn)      | Late summer (Earth) | Dusk       |
| End       | C1 to C3          | Rubedo (reddening: completion, the Stone)   | Autumn (Metal)    | Night        |
| Post-game | ∞                 | (beyond the Work)                           | Winter (Water)    | A new day    |

The Great Work fits unusually well: Albedo's purification lands on the Precision Age's cleanrooms, Citrinitas (the "solar" stage) on the Fusion Age and Celestial magic, and Rubedo, the stage that makes the Philosopher's Stone, on the combined tiers.

## Mixing

- Keep familiar names early (Stone, Bronze, Steam) where players expect them, and switch to invented ones from the Electric or Industrial Age on, as the world gets stranger.
- Or drop "Age" entirely and use bare names (the Crucible, the Athanor, the Oracle), which read more like places than history.
- A favorite combination: blend names for eras, Great Work stages for the ranges. Both come from alchemy, so the magic track is in the names even for players who ignore it until it's required.

## Current pick

Each row has a **shared era name** (Hearth, Crucible...) for the row as a whole, used for the quest book section and the era briefing, and each track keeps its **own tier name** (Stone Age, Folk Magic) for its tab. The shared names are the alchemy and early science words from the blend column above, which fit both tracks:

- **Hearth**: the first fire, folk charms.
- **Crucible**: where metals are alloyed and essences first pulled out.
- **Clockwork**: belts, and glyphs that run on their own.
- **Galvanic**: Galvani's frog legs, electricity that looked like magic.
- **Alembic**: the alchemist's still: distillation on both tracks.
- **Athanor**: the alchemist's furnace, built to hold a steady heat. Heat and infusion strength.
- **Prism**: lenses, wafers, pure light, clean rooms.
- **Nexus**: the reactor core and the ley line nexus, both placement puzzles around a dangerous center.
- **Oracle**: computers that know things, and divination.
- **Zenith**: the top of the sky.
- **Sunforge**: a star held in a reactor, a great spirit held in a circle.
- **Paradox**, **Magnum Opus**, **Exodus**, **Horizon**: the combined tiers and the post-game.

# Power Grade Names

Alternatives to the flow names picked in [Tech Branch](Tech%20Branch.md#power-grades). Worth keeping as alternatives, or for other systems that need tiers (magic, cables, belts).

| Scheme | Example run | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Celestial** | Spark, Ember, Candle, Lantern, Beacon, Flare, Dawn, Sun, Giant, Nova, Supernova, Pulsar, Quasar, Magnetar, Singularity | Fits the space and ship story. Escalates well. | Light, not power, and the middle order (Dawn vs Sun vs Giant) is less obvious. **A great fit for magic grades instead**, see [Magic Branch](Magic%20Branch.md). |
| **Escalating adjectives** | Low, Medium, High, Extreme, Insane, Ludicrous... | The GregTech joke, instantly familiar. | Too close to GregTech to feel original. |
| **Roman numerals** | Grade I, II, III... XV | Clear, neutral, no naming work. | No personality. |
| **Era names** | Steam, Electric, Industrial... | Ties grades directly to eras. | Locks grades to eras forever, and the names get long. |
| **Materials** | Copper, Steel, Aluminum, Titanium... | Matches cable tiers. | Collides with actual material names in recipes. |
| **Music dynamics** | Pianissimo, Piano, Mezzo-piano, Mezzo-forte, Forte, Fortissimo... | Charming. | Runs out at about 8, and most players won't know the order. |
| **Storm scale** | Breeze, Gust, Gale, Storm, Squall, Typhoon, Hurricane... | Intuitive, like flow names. | A bit shorter, and wind is less natural for electricity. |
