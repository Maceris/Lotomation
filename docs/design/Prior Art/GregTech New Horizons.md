# Machines

- Arc Furnace (normal, plasma) - Recycles machines and components back into Ingots
- Assembler - Crafts things automatically
- Assembly Line - Makes advanced machines
- Autoclave - Crystallizes dusts
- Bending Machine - Bends things into plates, springs, foil, rails, cans
- Boiler (small steam, high pressure steam, steam solar, small steam lava, high pressure steam lava) - Produces steam from water and fuel
- Blast Furnace - Heats up ingots, dopes boules, makes certain alloys, smelts, engraves some things
- Brewery - Turns things into juice, potions, drinks, various fluids
- Canner - Cans things
- Centrifuge - Centrifuges out dusts, pulls fluids/gasses/dusts out of things
- Chemical Bath - Bathes ores in chemicals to separate them, imbues things with fluids, dying, coating
- Circuit Assembler - Builds circuits
- Coke Oven - Processes logs and coal into charcoal and coal coke
- Compressor - Compresses things (ingots → blocks, plants → plant balls, some dusts into lumps/ingots/blocks)
- Cutting Machine - Cuts things (planks → slabs, rods → bolts, pressure plate → button, rod → bolt)
- Cyclotron - Accelerates particles to 80% light speed, creates pellets from liquids, breaks apart atoms, and plasma into atoms
- Dehydrator - Dehydrates foods, breaks certain dusts down into components
- Distillery - Distills fluids down to refined versions, might have byproducts
- Dimensionally Transcendent Plasma Forge - Used for generating molten infinity and dimensionally transcendent residue
- Electrolyzer - Splits up dusts and fluids using electrolysis
- Electromagnetic Separator - Separates magnetic ores from the rest
- Extractor - Extracts the essence or juice of things (rubber, special tree parts, dyes, juices, blocks to ingots)
- Fermenter - Ferments fluids
- Fishing Pond - Generates fish
- Fluid Extractor - Extracts fluids, sometimes leaving ashes or something behind
- Fluid Heater - Heats up fluids, melts certain dusts
- Fluid Solidifier - Casts fluids using molds
- Forge Hammer - Crushes tools and machines into dust, acts as a hammer for things like making plates and long rods
- Forming Press - Imprints images into things, presses them into molds, presses things like plates and screws together
- Furnace - Smelts things
- Fusion Reactor - Performs fusion reactions of liquids/gasses/plasmas
- Laser Engraver - Uses lenses to fix flawed gems, etch things (blocks, circuits)
- Lathe - Shaves things down like a file (ingots/gem → rod + dust, plate → lens + dust)
- Macerator - Crushes up things
- Matter Amplifier - Turns scrap and scrap boxes into UU Amplifier
- Mass Fabricator - Turns UU Amplifier into UU Matter
- Miner - Mines ores for you
- Mixer - Blends things together, mixes dusts and/or fluids, makes smoothies
- Nano Forge - Creates nanites
- Nuclear Salt Processing Plant - Processes depleted nuclear salts from teh LFTR
- Ore Washer - Washes dusts into more pure versions and byproducts
- Packager - Puts things into boxes, combines things
- PCB Factory - Makes printed circuit boards
- Polarizer - Magnetizes things
- Printer - Prints punchcards, books
- Pump - Pumps fluids like water
- Replicator - Turns UU Matter into things described by a pattern
- Rock Breaker - Turns lava into stone/cobblestone, redstone → obsidian
- Sifter - Sifts through dusts for shards, various qualities of gems, more refined dusts
- Slicer - Slices food, circuits
- Teleporter - Teleports long distances
- Thermal Centrifuge - Splits up ores more precisely
- Tree Growth Simulator - Converts RF to logs
- Unpackager - Takes things out of boxes, splits things
- Vacuum Freezer - Cools hot ingots and cells
- Wire Mill - Extrudes wires
